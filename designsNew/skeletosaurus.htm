This Dinosaur originally looked very different, its name translated in Latin to "eternal Reflection", with various lines and shapes meant to illustrate this concept. However, after years of decay the dinosaur was given a new name Skeletosaurus and paint job by Greater Pittsburgh Orthopedic Associates. Skeletosaurus can be found in front of the business in Natrona Heights.
